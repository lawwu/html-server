Confidence changes the flow

  • Under 60%: flagged low. Nothing is pre-filled and Accept is off, so you label it yourself.
  • 60–79%: the suggestion is pre-filled. Change anything freely.
  • 80% or more: pre-filled. Overriding the sentiment asks for a one-key reason, which goes to the model team.

Guidelines v2.3, short version

  • Label the reviewer’s overall verdict. A mixed review takes the sentiment of its conclusion; no verdict is neutral.
  • Tag an aspect only when the review says something about it.
  • Star ratings are hidden, and agreement with the model shows only at the end of the batch, so neither can steer you.
