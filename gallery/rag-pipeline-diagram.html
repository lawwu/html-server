RAG pipeline — from documents to cited answers

Two paths share one index. Each night, changed documents are cleaned, chunked and embedded into it; each question is embedded, matched against it, reranked and handed to a language model with the six best chunks. A feedback loop of thumbs, golden questions and retrieval hit rate decides what to tune.
