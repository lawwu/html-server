Three optimizers, one valley

Training a model means walking downhill on a loss surface. Here the surface has only two parameters, so you can see it. Click anywhere to drop all three optimizers at the same spot, then press run. Push the learning rate up until something breaks.

Gradient descentθ ← θ − η∇L(θ)
Step straight downhill. In a narrow valley the steepest direction points across it, not along it, so GD zig-zags.
Momentumv ← βv + ∇L; θ ← θ − ηv
Keep a running velocity. Side-to-side gradients cancel; the along-the-valley component accumulates.
Adamθ ← θ − α·m̂ / (√v̂ + ε)
Rescale each coordinate by its recent gradient size, so steep and shallow directions take similar-sized steps.
